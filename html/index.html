What is Sustainability?

The definition of sustainability as defined by the United Nations Brundtland Commision is "meeting the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs"

It has also been defined as "the balance between the environment, equity, and economy" by the University of California, Los Angeles.

In simple terms, it refers to the ability to maintain or support a process continuously over time.

Why is Sustainability important?

Sustainability is important for the health of our planet and the well-being of not only the present but also future generations.

Sustainability allows for individuals and businesses to address environmental challenges such as climate change and pollution which have become increasingly unpredictable and dangerous.

Climate change, driven by human activities, has dire consequences on the Earth, destroying ecosystems, threatening biodiversity and disrupting human societies across the globe.

What is Climate Action?

Climate action refers to the efforts being taken to combat climate change and mitigate its impacts. In recent years, due to human activity, the climate has become increasingly unstable, a trend that poses serious risks to both people and the planet.

In order to slow down these effects, we must act immediately.

Climate action can take many forms, including strategies, policies, and large-scale initiatives. It can also begin with individuals making smarter, more sustainable choices in their everyday lives.

Why is Climate Action important?

Climate action is important in order to slow down the impacts of climate change. If we do not act, the conseuqnces will be dire.

We have already begun to feel these effects firsthand such as rising global temperatures, melting ice caps, more frequent extreme weather events, and steadily increasing sea levels. These not only threaten natural ecosystems but also impact human health, agriculture, and the stability of communities around the word.

This has lead to communties and countries around the world to start taking action. One such example is the United Nations Sustainability Development Goals (UN SDGs) goal 13, Taking urgent action to combat climate change and its impact.
